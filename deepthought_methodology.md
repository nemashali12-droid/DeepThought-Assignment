# Methodology Document — DeepThought ICP Research
## Ahmedabad | Specialty Biotech + Diagnostics / Life Science Tools

---

## 1. City and Segment Selection Rationale

**City: Ahmedabad**

Ahmedabad is one of India's strongest manufacturing hubs for biotech and life sciences. Key reasons:

- **GIDC industrial estates** (Naroda, Narol, Changodar, Sanand) concentrate pharma, biotech, and chemical manufacturers in one corridor
- **Sarkhej-Gandhinagar corridor** (S.G. Highway) is home to the headquarters of Zydus, Intas, Troikaa, Hester, and dozens of mid-size specialty players
- **Gujarat State Biotechnology Mission (GSBTM)** provides government support, grants, and incubation to biotech companies — creating a pipeline of emerging specialty manufacturers
- **GIDC Chandkheda and Vatva** have older chemical/biotech clusters with established manufacturers
- **IIT Gandhinagar** (15km from Ahmedabad) provides a talent and spin-off pipeline that increasingly seeds deep-tech biotech companies
- Ahmedabad is NOT the same city as Hyderabad (DeepThought's own research city) — this gives original research value to this submission

**Segment: Specialty Biotech + Diagnostics & Life Science Tools (Basket A)**

These two segments were combined because in Ahmedabad they overlap substantially — many diagnostic companies also manufacture research-grade reagents and life science tools. Specific reasons for selection:

- Both are Basket A (strongest ICP fit per the brief)
- Ahmedabad has a genuine cluster in IVD diagnostics (Angstrom Biotech, Athenese Dx, Marck Biosciences), specialty biotech/fermentation (Concord Biotech, Gujarat Enzyme, Aumgene nearby in Surat), and emerging life science tools (OmniBRx, GeNext Genomics)
- These segments have strong Make-in-India tailwinds (PLI for medical devices; DSIR recognition program; government genomics initiative)
- China+1 is directly applicable: India imports >70% of IVD reagents and life science tools; domestic manufacturers benefit from import substitution policies

## 2. Research Process and Sources Used

### Step 1: Build Initial Universe (Target: 75–100 companies)

**Sources used:**

1. **pharmchoices.com / built-in.com lists** — "List of Biotech Companies in Ahmedabad, Gujarat 2025" gave a starting company directory with addresses
2. **Tracxn.com** — used for company profiles, revenue, founding year, investors, employee count for key names (Concord Biotech, OmniBRx, Hester, etc.)
3. **Tofler.in / Zauba Corp** — MCA financials, director details, CIN numbers; used to verify revenue, incorporated date, and ownership structure
4. **Glassdoor / LinkedIn** — used to find hiring patterns, company size, and identify management background
5. **Company websites** — primary source for: products manufactured, certifications claimed (ISO, DSIR, FDA, CE), R&D capabilities, and founder backgrounds
6. **Google search variants:**
   - `Ahmedabad specialty biotech diagnostics manufacturer DSIR`
   - `Ahmedabad diagnostic kit manufacturer IVD USFDA`
   - `Ahmedabad enzyme probiotic fermentation company Gujarat`
   - `OmniBRx founder revenue` / `Hester Biosciences revenue 2024`
7. **Wikipedia / Grokipedia** — for verifying founding stories (Concord Biotech was particularly detailed on Grokipedia)
8. **Crunchbase / PitchBook** — funding data, investor information, valuation
9. **Pitchbook TTM revenue data** — used to confirm Concord Biotech crossed Rs.500Cr
10. **ADMI (Association of Diagnostics Manufacturers of India)** website for IVD sector context

### Step 2: Apply Auto-Disqualify Filters

Each company was passed through the hard filters first:

| Filter | Companies removed in this research |
|--------|-----------------------------------|
| Revenue > Rs.500Cr | Concord Biotech (Rs.1240Cr); Intas; Zydus; Cadila; Sun Pharma; Eris; Alembic; Angstrom (borderline — verify) |
| Subsidiary of large group | Adani Pharmachem; Syngene; Biocon unit; Excel (now FMC-owned); Claris (now Baxter) |
| PE/VC-controlled | Concord Biotech (Quadria Capital historically; now listed — promoter owns ~40%) |
| Service company (CRO/testing lab) | Lambda Therapeutic; Veeda Clinical Research; Vimta Labs |
| Generic pharma | Zydus; Sun Pharma; Cadila; Alembic |
| No website | Multiple unlisted companies removed |
| Wrong city | Aumgene (Surat — borderline; flagged for verification); Windlas (Haridwar) |

### Step 3: Score Remaining Companies (Federer Score)

For each company that passed auto-disqualify filters, each of the 6 criteria was scored Weak/Moderate/Strong with one line of evidence:

- **C1 (Manufacturer):** Verified via website product pages, plant descriptions, certifications (FDA, ISO 13485 for medical devices/IVD)
- **C2 (India-based):** Address verification via Zauba Corp / MCA; physical plant location vs registered office
- **C3 (Differentiated):** Looked for: patents (searched on Tracxn, company website, Google Patent); regulatory approvals (USFDA, EU-GMP, DSIR, CE); "first/only" claims; technical product descriptions
- **C4 (Technical DM):** LinkedIn search for founders and MDs; Wikipedia; Grokipedia; company "About" pages; Crunchbase founder info
- **C5 (Growing sector):** Assessed based on segment + tailwind research (PLI scheme lists, Make-in-India initiative sectors, China+1 reports)
- **C6 (Growth signals):** LinkedIn job postings; company press sections; Tracxn news tab; MCA filing dates (recent balance sheet = activity signal)

### Step 4: Personalization Hook Generation

For each passing company, identified one specific, true, recent, and verifiable detail that could open an outreach email. Criteria for a good hook:
- Must be specific (not "they make diagnostic kits" but "they are one of fewer than 5 Gujarat IVD manufacturers with DSIR recognition")
- Must be recent (within 2–3 years)
- Must be relevant to growth or achievement
- Must be true (sourced from a verifiable document or press item)

## 3. What I Learned About the Segment

### Key findings about Ahmedabad's specialty biotech/diagnostics landscape:

**1. The city is dominated by large pharma — the specialty mid-market is smaller than expected**

Ahmedabad has a world-class pharma cluster but it skews large (Zydus, Intas, Sun Pharma, Alembic) or generic. Finding sub-Rs.500Cr specialty manufacturers requires going below the radar — MSME-scale companies, unlisted entities, and spin-offs. This is actually good for DeepThought's ICP (less competition from institutional buyers) but makes research harder.

**2. The diagnostics/IVD cluster is real but underexposed**

Ahmedabad has a genuine IVD manufacturing cluster (Angstrom, Athenese, Marck, Add-X, Arinna) that barely appears in national biotech coverage. These are the kinds of companies that have real technical depth but low public profiles — exactly the ICP. The ADMI (Association of Diagnostics Manufacturers of India) is a useful ongoing source for this segment.

**3. Life science tools manufacturing is nascent but growing rapidly**

OmniBRx, GeNext Genomics, and Agile Bioscience represent an emerging Ahmedabad cluster in life science tools — reagents, bioreactors, CRISPR tools. IIT Gandhinagar is a likely spin-off source for future companies in this space. Most of these companies are early-stage but strategically positioned for China+1.

**4. The CRO trap is real in Ahmedabad**

Ahmedabad has several well-known CROs (Veeda, Lambda) that look like manufacturers but are service companies. Lambda's "we manufacture" language on their website nearly passed initial scanning. Always verify the company makes a product, not a service.

**5. Revenue verification is critical — fast-growing companies cross Rs.500Cr quickly**

Concord Biotech was under Rs.500Cr as recently as 3 years ago. Angstrom Biotech may be near the limit today. Revenue data on Tracxn/Tofler lags by 1–2 years. For fast-growing companies, always verify the most recent revenue through MCA filings or news, not just the last annual report.

## 4. Research Limitations and Gaps

- **Founder background** was the hardest criterion to verify for unlisted companies. Many Ahmedabad SME founders do not have LinkedIn profiles or public bios. Several companies were flagged as Borderline specifically because C4 could not be verified remotely.
- **Revenue data** lags by 1–2 years on public databases. Angstrom Biotech and Troikaa require current revenue verification before finalizing as passes.
- **City boundary** is ambiguous for Surat-based companies (Aumgene) and Vadodara-based companies (Antozyme). The research team must make a judgment call — the assignment's guidance is "operational presence, not registered office" — for Surat companies, a direct call is needed to confirm if R&D, founders, or primary manufacturing are genuinely in Ahmedabad.
- **Newer companies** (post-2020 incorporations) have minimal public data. Add-X, Agile Bioscience, GeNext, and BioScan all need direct outreach to verify manufacturing and founder credentials.

## 6. What Would Have Made This Research Better

1. **Direct phone calls** to 15–20 companies to verify founder background, revenue, and manufacturing details that are not public
2. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** to find and verify founder profiles for unlisted companies
3. **ADMI membership directory** (paid access) for a complete list of certified IVD manufacturers in Gujarat
4. **MCA Pro access** (Tofler paid tier) for balance sheets filed within the last 12 months — this would resolve the revenue verification gap for companies like Angstrom and Troikaa
5. **GSBTM (Gujarat State Biotechnology Mission) list** of supported companies — this is a government dataset that would surface specialty biotech companies not yet in commercial databases
